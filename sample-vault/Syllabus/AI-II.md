# Syllabus — Artificial Intelligence II (UFCF9S-15-2)

## Module details
- Level: 5, Credits: 15
- Pre-requisite: UFCFGS-15-1 Artificial Intelligence I (or UFCEY7-10-1)

## Structure
The module splits into two halves: **Optimisation** (searching for a good
solution) and **Modelling** (learning a function from data).

## Assessment
- In-class test, 45 minutes, 100% weighting, first sit and resit same format.
- A third of the test covers experimental/tutorial work.
- Question formats: short essays, multiple-choice questions, problem-solving
  exercises.

## Learning outcomes
- MO1: Compare and contrast modern AI techniques with traditional approaches.
- MO2: Identify issues in applying modern AI, including ethical issues, and
  evaluate the challenges.
- MO3: Select appropriate paradigms and solve problems with AI techniques.
